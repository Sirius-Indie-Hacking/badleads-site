Support Bad Leads is a detective game about Kip, a broke private eye who takes the one case nobody else wants. Talk to the people in the building, collect clues and work out who did it. Contact us Something broken, unclear or missing? Write to contact@badleads.xyz . Tell us which phone you use and what happened, and we will reply as soon as we can. Report an ad Saw an ad that seems inappropriate or not suitable for teens? Write to contact@badleads.xyz with the subject "Report an ad". Tell us roughly when you saw it and what the ad showed (a screenshot helps). We review every report and block the ad in AdMob where we can. You can also tap the small "i" icon inside the ad to see why Google showed it and to send feedback to Google. Start the case over Tap the sliders button in the top right corner of the game.
